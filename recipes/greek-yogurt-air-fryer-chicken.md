# Greek Yogurt Marinated Air Fryer Chicken

Juicy-inside, crispy-outside chicken breasts — the Greek yogurt marinade tenderizes while the air fryer crisps. Minimal effort, weeknight-friendly.

**Source:** [@carlys_cookbook](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeHzyP7A_uA/) · Oct 2026

## Marinade

- 1 1/2 lbs boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 3/4 cup plain Greek yogurt
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 1/2 tsp dried oregano
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 tsp kosher salt
- 1/2 tsp black pepper
- 1/2 tsp onion powder

## Method

1. Whisk everything except the chicken into a marinade.
2. Coat the chicken and refrigerate at least 30 minutes, up to 8 hours.
3. Preheat the air fryer to 400°F.
4. Shake off excess marinade. Air fry 10–14 minutes, flipping halfway, until the thickest part hits 165°F.
5. Rest 5 minutes, then slice.

## Notes

- Marinating past 8 hours? Leave the lemon juice out of the marinade and squeeze it on fresh right before cooking — long acid exposure messes with the texture.
- The yogurt is doing the work here: it clings better than an oil marinade, so you get a real crust in the air fryer instead of steaming.
