# Brown Sugar Pop-Tart Latte

Iced shaken-espresso latte that tastes like a brown sugar cinnamon Pop-Tart — warm, sweet, with a hint of sea salt. Oat milk keeps it creamy.

**Source:** [@gobbleacoffee](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DeVAi7fPb1H/) · Oct 2026

## Shaken espresso

- 2 shots espresso
- Brown sugar (to taste — start with ~1 tbsp)
- Cinnamon (a good pinch)
- Drizzle of maple syrup
- Pinch of flaky sea salt

Shake hard until nice and foamy.

## Assemble

1. Fill a glass with ice.
2. Add oat milk (about 2/3 full).
3. Top with the shaken espresso.

## Notes

- The brown sugar + maple + cinnamon is the Pop-Tart flavor; the sea salt keeps it from going flat-sweet.
- Works with regular milk too, but oat milk's creaminess sells the pastry vibe.
